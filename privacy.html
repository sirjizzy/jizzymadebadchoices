Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the [Brand Name] publishing tool (the "App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is used to manage and publish content to [Brand Name]'s own TikTok account.

1. Information we collect

When you connect a TikTok account to the App, we receive only the information needed to publish content on your behalf:

  • Basic TikTok profile information, such as your display name, avatar, and TikTok open ID, provided through TikTok's authorization process.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, which allow the App to publish to the connected account. We never see or store your TikTok password.
  • Content you choose to publish, such as videos, photos, captions, and related post settings.

We do not collect information about your followers, your private messages, or your activity on TikTok beyond what is listed above.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • Authenticate the connected TikTok account.
  • Upload and publish the content you request.
  • Display basic account details within the App so you can confirm which account is connected.
  • Keep the App secure and working properly.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Sharing of information

Content and account information is shared with TikTok solely to carry out the publishing actions you request, in line with TikTok's own Privacy Policy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Data storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to operate the App. Uploaded content is kept only as long as needed to complete publishing. If you revoke the App's access or ask us to delete your data, we will delete stored tokens and related information within 30 days, unless we are legally required to keep it.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we handle. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You can revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings under "Manage app permissions." Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object to its processing. To make a request, contact us using the email below.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy
